# Non-author review of Insider — 2026-10-27

Reviewer: Partner. Independently reopened all six original URLs below and inspected the relevant passages. No game-client testing or authenticated API-documentation inspection occurred.

- **INS-1 — Supported for automated gameplay and account-sanction risk; adviser exemption unresolved.** The currently served [Terms of Service](https://supercell.com/en/terms-of-service/) have effective date 6 November 2024. Section 1.1's use limitations explicitly list “automation software, emulators, bots” within prohibited conduct. The [Safe and Fair Play policy](https://supercell.com/en/safe-and-fair-play/) explicitly lists bots/gameplay automation services/scripts and says prohibited third-party unfair-advantage software results in permanent bans. The [30 April 2024 Clash announcement](https://supercell.com/en/games/clashofclans/blog/misc/update-to-our-fair-play-policy-and-next-steps/) announces regular bot ban waves and permanent banning of accounts identified as bots. Autonomous screen-driven deployment is covered by the ordinary meaning of gameplay automation even without modifying game files; this last classification is an inference. The sources do not provide an explicit safe harbor for offline screenshot advisers or live suggestion overlays. Equally, absence of an exemption is not evidence that every offline analysis tool is prohibited. Say unclarified, not categorically approved/prohibited. These sources establish contractual/account consequences, not a criminal-law conclusion or certainty that any particular account will be detected.

- **INS-2 — Supported qualification.** The [10 October 2023 official PC announcement](https://supercell.com/en/news/google-play-pc-soft-launch/) explicitly says Supercell will officially support Clash of Clans on PC through Google Play Games, including mouse controls. This prevents a categorical claim that PC play or all Android-on-PC execution is forbidden. It does not authorize bots or establish permission for arbitrary emulators. Prefer “Use the officially supported Google Play Games release for manual PC play; automation remains separately prohibited.” The source is a soft-launch announcement and alone does not establish current regional availability or hardware requirements.

- **INS-7 — Supported as historical evidence only.** The [30 March 2020 release notes](https://supercell.com/en/games/clashofclans/blog/news/the-spring-update-is-here-2/) discuss “hidden objects such as Tesla's and Traps” and changes to their visibility in same-clan profile previews. This proves hidden-state concepts existed and that visibility depended on viewing context. It is not a complete 2026 scouting-visibility specification and does not establish current Clan Castle visibility, exact trap inventories, or how every mode exposes them. Preserve the historical label; current-client evidence is needed for current exhaustive claims.

- **INS-4 — Supported limitation; endpoint absence unverified.** Opening the [official API landing page](https://developer.clashofclans.com/) yielded only an iframe reference in the research tool, with no endpoint catalog. It cannot establish the absence of live tactical, base-layout, simulation, or control endpoints. Correct wording is “We could not verify an official tactical/simulation interface from the accessible public API page.” Do not turn search failure or an inaccessible JavaScript page into an endpoint-absence claim.

No substantive disagreement remains after these scope qualifications. The strongest verified product constraint is the explicit automation prohibition; offline-adviser policy and current comprehensive visibility/API details remain unresolved.
